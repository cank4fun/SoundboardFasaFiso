Third-party notices
===================

miniaudio
---------
SoundBoardFasaFiso includes miniaudio by David Reid.

The vendored miniaudio header offers a choice between the Unlicense
(public domain dedication) and the MIT No Attribution license. The complete
license text is preserved at third_party/miniaudio/miniaudio.h in the source
repository.

RNNoise
-------
SoundBoardFasaFiso includes RNNoise v0.2 by Xiph.Org contributors for
microphone noise suppression.

RNNoise is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License. The complete license
text is preserved at third_party/rnnoise/COPYING in the source repository.
The vendored spike uses the upstream little model from model version 0b50c45.


WebRTC Audio Processing (optional build)
----------------------------------------
AEC-enabled builds statically link the WebRTC Audio Processing Module and its
transitive vcpkg dependencies. Their complete notices are generated from the
pinned manifest installation and shipped separately as
WEBRTC_THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt in the portable package.
